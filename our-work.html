Engineering Atmosphere

For Nebula OS we rebuilt the entire spatial interface layer from scratch — a real-time 3D environment that responds to touch, light and motion simultaneously. We spent six weeks on physics alone before touching visual design.

The result is a system that feels less like software and more like a place. Every surface has weight. Every transition has intention. It shipped to 2.4 million users on day one with zero reported accessibility failures.
