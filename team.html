How We Work

Culture
Eats Process

We do not have a playbook. We have taste. Every project starts with a genuine curiosity about the problem and ends when the work feels right — not when the sprint ends.

The team is distributed across Lisbon, Berlin and Tokyo. We meet in person twice a year for deep-work sprints and ship the rest of the time asynchronously — which means our written communication is very good.

Join the Studio
Senior Only

Everyone on the team has 8+ years in their discipline. There are no juniors to manage, no accounts team to appease — just practitioners doing the work they know best.

Full Ownership

Each person owns a domain end-to-end. Lena owns design. Marcus owns engineering. Nobody reviews or approves each other's core work — trust is the operating model.

Ship It

We have a strong bias toward finishing. A launched product that is 95% right is worth ten that never escaped a Figma file. We iterate in public, not in private.

Selective Work

We take three or four projects a year. That means when we are in, we are fully in — not splitting attention across twelve clients and three time zones simultaneously.
