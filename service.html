Our Process

How We
Work

We do not run cookie-cutter sprints. Every engagement is shaped around the specific problem and the team we are working with. That said, we do follow a consistent rhythm.

Most projects move through four phases: Discover, Define, Design, Deliver. Each phase has clear inputs and outputs, and we share work continuously — never disappearing for three weeks only to reappear with a big reveal.

01
Discover

Stakeholder interviews, competitive audit, user research. We build a shared understanding of the problem before touching any tool.

1–2 weeks
02
Define

Strategy brief, information architecture, creative direction. We align on the north star and get explicit agreement before design work begins.

1 week
03
Design

Iterative design and prototyping in weekly cycles. Live Figma, recorded reviews, async feedback loops — no waiting for the big reveal.

4–12 weeks
04
Deliver

Handoff-ready specs, design system documentation, engineering support and QA review at launch. We stay close until it ships right.

1–2 weeks
